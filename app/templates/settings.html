Playlist source health
Checks enabled Live, VOD, Series and Local playlist items every 30 seconds. Missing links, empty commands, disabled portals, banned/expired accounts, missing episode fallbacks and missing/unreadable files are reported. Busy accounts and recorded connection failures are warnings. Unverified does not mean working. Recent media-probe/playback evidence lasts 15 minutes in this server process. Local availability checks do not verify decoding. Automatic checks never open streams; Probe source is an explicit, bounded check before FFmpeg. Input catalogue selection flags alone do not disable explicit playback links. Disabled playlist items are excluded.

EPG sources and matching
EPG guide health Missing means no usable programme in the next 48 hours. A current gap means future data exists but nothing covers now. Stale means all contributing feeds are older than their stale threshold. Checks use the same priorities, gap filling and time corrections as XMLTV. Alerts refresh every 30 seconds and do not send email or browser notifications.
Checking guide coverage…
Only a single confident match is assigned automatically to an empty channel. Ambiguous matches and replacements open a review dialog; existing IDs are not silently overwritten. After changing IDs, cached guide data is reprocessed. For external players, open Users → output URLs and copy EPG (xmltv). It includes enabled Live channels in that user's selected groups.
