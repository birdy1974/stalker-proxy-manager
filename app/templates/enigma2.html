Receivers
A profile turns the playlist into Enigma2 userbouquet.*.tv files. VOD & series get subtitles only when their player is 5002 (ServiceApp/exteplayer3) and the container is mkv.
Receiver
Players & containers. The leading number of the service reference decides which player the box uses; the container decides which URL alias it gets. MPEG-TS cannot carry text subtitles, so VOD and series need mkv + 5002; live TV is best served as ts (native DVB subtitles).
The receiver. With transport ftp SPM uploads the bouquets itself (atomic rename, bouquets.tv backed up as a restore point) and reloads the service list over OpenWebif; download means you use the installer one-liner below instead.
Install on the box (paste into telnet/SSH — re-run it any time to refresh)
The one-liner downloads the bouquets, backs up bouquets.tv, merges our entries into it (yours are kept) and reloads the service list via OpenWebif. It carries a per-profile token, not your admin password.
